## What

A car with a stereo camera and no starting position localizes itself on a 20 km by 20 km (400 km²) aerial map around Karlsruhe, Germany. While it runs, the method reads only stereo images, camera calibration, and timestamps: no GPS and no IMU.

The map comes from 20 cm aerial orthophotos (LGL-BW). Its layers (roads, buildings, trees) are segmented from that imagery automatically. Stereo visual odometry tracks the car's motion, and matching what the camera sees (road shape, walls, tree trunks) against those layers places the car on the map, then keeps it there as it drives.

## Where it stands

Experiments use KITTI and KITTI-360. The paper is in preparation with my advisor, Prof. Kaveh Fathian, at the ARIA Lab at Mines, targeting IEEE RA-L.
