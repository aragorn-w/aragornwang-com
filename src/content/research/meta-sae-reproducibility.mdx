## What

A sparse autoencoder (SAE) trained on a language model's activations learns a dictionary of latents. A meta-SAE is a second sparse autoencoder trained on the first one's decoder rows, and meta-SAEs can be stacked, each level trained on the one below. This project stacks them up to five levels deep and asks one question: do the latents a meta-SAE learns come back when only the training seed changes?

## How

Agreement across seeds is measured against matched-noise baselines. The study covers 12 first-level SAEs on two language models, Gemma-2-2B and GPT-2 Small, and compares stacked dictionaries with dictionaries trained directly on the first-level decoder rows.

## Why

Reproducibility across seeds helps show whether learned latents reflect stable structure in the model or variation between training runs.
