- Developed an augmented reality app for viewing full-scale 3D models with data on all model components
- Extended app capabilities to allow an infinitely expandable peer-to-peer network
- Publicly spoke and presented the app to a U.S. Air Force Major General, the Executive Vice President of Lockheed Martin Space, and multiple other program executives
- Final product was presented to the Lockheed Martin board of directors
- Project was handed off to a full-time team, used by the Pentagon to show military products to defense industry partners
