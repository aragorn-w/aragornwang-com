Ongoing undergraduate research with the Autonomy, Robotics, & Intelligent Algorithms Lab at Mines, advised by Prof. Kaveh Fathian. My first paper, in preparation for IEEE RA-L, localizes a ground vehicle over a 400 km² search area by matching what its stereo camera sees against automatically segmented aerial maps.

- Researching global localization of ground vehicles that reads only stereo images, calibration, and timestamps while running (no GPS, no IMU): matching road shape, walls, and tree trunks against layers segmented automatically from 20 cm aerial orthophotos places the vehicle on the map with no starting position and keeps it there
- Presented an earlier, monocular version of the work at the 2026 MURF Symposium: 1st Place Oral Presentation, selected from 27 teams
